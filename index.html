About Me

Hi, I’m Taya Dicho, a web designer and developer passionate about creating modern, responsive websites that help individuals and businesses grow online.

I specialize in designing clean, user-focused websites — from personal portfolios to business websites and custom web experiences. Every project is crafted with attention to detail, ensuring it looks professional, communicates clearly, and works seamlessly on all devices.

My approach combines simplicity, structure, and performance. I focus on layouts, typography, and interactions that make websites both beautiful and functional.

I believe every website should reflect the person or brand behind it. By understanding your ideas, goals, and audience, I turn them into a reliable and visually strong website tailored to your needs.
